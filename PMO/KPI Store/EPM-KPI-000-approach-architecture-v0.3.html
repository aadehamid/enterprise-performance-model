Workstream 1 · KPI Store · Approach and Architecture

One definition,
two computation paths

The KPI Store must serve leadership a frozen, certified number and simultaneously let an AI agent ask a question nobody precomputed. That is two execution modes over one certified formula — compiled only by the consumption semantic layer (Databricks Metric Views for the programme; MetricFlow for demo 1 only). Gold is a versioned snapshot of that compile, not a second formula. Meaning (ontology) and formula (Store catalog + consumption semantic layer) must agree; they are not one object. C-10 is unchanged — only the compiler tool changed.

The expectation to manage early

"We extracted all the KPIs from all the reports" is almost certainly "we extracted all the calculations." In a migrated Tableau-to-Power BI estate, the large majority will classify as presentation-only formatting, technical helpers, duplicates and near-duplicates, or local report measures. A realistic expectation is that a minority survive classification as enterprise KPIs. Saying this before the classification results land is straightforward; saying it after looks like an excuse.

02The tier-elevation frame

A useful way to describe what this workstream actually does. Everything extracted starts at Tier 8. The project's job is to move a selected subset up to Tier 1.

TierStateWhat moves it up
8Unvalidated report logic. Where every extracted calculation sits today, regardless of how widely used it wasNormalisation and classification
6–7Working analysis. Classified and contextualised (domain IRIs, Constraint references, data lineage) — but still our analysis, not their agreement. Process linkage may explain why a number exists; it does not elevateSME review
5Candidate KPI under formal review. In the Candidate KPI Register with a proposed owner, definition, target, thresholdStore client validation (KPI-020) + a formal certification act
1–3Approved enterprise KPI. In the Store catalog with an accountable owner and an approved definition—
Two rules this frame enforces

Report popularity is not evidence of anything. A calculation appearing in forty dashboards is a Tier 8 calculation appearing in forty dashboards. It tells you about consumption, not about governance.
A review is not an approval. The SME review and Store client validation (KPI-020) are both evidence steps. Tier 1 requires a governance act by a named accountable owner — a signature, a forum decision, a recorded approval. Without that, the catalog is a well-researched list.

Why this framing is worth using out loud It gives the client a clear, non-defensive answer to "why can't we just publish what we extracted?" The answer is not "your calculations are bad." It is "these are at tier eight, here is what each step upward buys you, and here is what it costs." It reframes governance as value rather than as friction.

04The dual-path problem

The central architectural issue. One KPI definition, two execution paths, two results — unless the design forces them to agree and proves it continuously.

Path A

Certified Gold — snapshot of the compile

The same Databricks Metric Views compile (programme) is executed on a schedule; the result is written, versioned, effective-dated, and frozen. Leadership consumes the snapshot. Gold does not own a second formula. Demo 1 uses the MetricFlow compile.

Guarantees: reproducible, auditable, restatement-controlled, fast, stable under re-query.
Cannot do: answer a question at a grain or slice nobody anticipated; reflect data that arrived after the load.

Path B

Consumption semantic layer, ad hoc

The same certified formula is compiled by Databricks Metric Views to SQL at query time and run against the Databricks SQL warehouse (Azure account for the actual project; Free edition for demo 2 only). Business users and AI agents consume it. This is the only programme compile path. MetricFlow compiles demo 1 only.

Guarantees: current data, arbitrary slicing within declared limits, answers questions nobody precomputed.
Cannot do: be the number of record; guarantee that today's answer matches last month's board pack.

Where the two paths diverge — and they will

#Divergence sourceWhy it happensSeverity
DV-1As-of timeGold was computed at load time; the ad hoc query runs later against data that has movedHigh
DV-2Late-arriving and corrected source dataA source correction lands after the Gold load. Gold holds the old truth by design; ad hoc sees the new oneHigh
DV-3Definition version driftThe Gold row was computed under definition v1; the compiler now holds v2. Both are correct; they are not the same numberHigh
DV-4Non-additive measures aggregated across grainGold stores at a chosen grain. Ad hoc aggregates across it. You cannot sum a ratio, average an average, or add distinct counts. The most common and most damaging sourceCritical
DV-5Filter application orderA filter applied before aggregation and one applied after produce different answers for anything non-additiveHigh
DV-6Conversion pointCurrency and unit-of-measure conversion applied at different stages or dates. In Downstream, volume at ambient versus standard temperature is the classic caseHigh
DV-7Null and exception handlingPrecompute may default or exclude; ad hoc may propagate nulls into the denominatorMedium
DV-8Row-level securitySecurity applied at different points changes the denominator. Two users legitimately see two different "totals"Medium
DV-9Time-period boundary semanticsFiscal versus calendar, period-to-date behaviour, timezone of the transaction dateMedium

The reconciliation contract

Six commitments that make the two paths a single architecture rather than two systems with a shared vocabulary. Tier 9 — proposed.

#CommitmentWhat it means in practice
RC-1One certified formula, one compile pathThe programme compiler is Databricks Metric Views (Unity Catalog Business Semantics). Gold is a versioned, immutable snapshot of that compile — not a second formula and not a formula SoT. MetricFlow is the compiler of demo 1 only (o2c-unbilled-semantic-layer), not the programme default. Meaning lives in the ontology; the formula lives in the Store catalog + consumption semantic layer. They must agree (same name, population, grain, bindings). They are not one object. If a batch job contains SQL that is not the Databricks Metric View compile (except demo 1), the architecture has already failed
RC-2The reproducibility assertionGiven the same as-of timestamp and the same definition version, the consumption semantic layer must reproduce the Gold snapshot exactly. Not approximately — exactly, or to a stated and justified tolerance. Same compile, both modes
RC-3RC-2 is a continuous test, not a design claimA regression suite runs both paths over a sample of KPIs and slices on every load and every definition change. Divergence is a build failure, not a discussion
RC-4Every answer carries its provenanceEvery Gold row and every ad hoc result carries definition version, as-of timestamp, and computation run reference. A user comparing two numbers can see immediately whether they are comparing like with like
RC-5Additivity is declared, and the compiler enforces itEach measure declares its aggregation behaviour and its legal aggregation paths. The compiler refuses to aggregate a ratio by summing rather than silently producing a wrong number. This single rule prevents the most damaging divergence class
RC-6Published-number precedence for leadershipFor any certified KPI in a governance or leadership context, Gold is the number of record because it is a frozen snapshot of the Databricks Metric Views compile (programme) — not because Gold owns the formula. Demo 1 Gold is a snapshot of the MetricFlow compile. The consumption semantic layer is for exploration and for questions the snapshot cannot answer. A discrepancy is a defect to investigate, never a matter of preference. Gold is not a formula SoT
The failure mode this prevents

Leadership's dashboard shows 94.2%. The AI agent, asked the same question, says 93.8%. Both are computed from "the same certified KPI." Nobody can say which is right, and the credibility of the entire KPI Store is gone in one meeting. This is not a hypothetical — it is the default outcome of building two compile paths, or of treating Gold as a second formula, without RC-2 and RC-6.

What the dual path buys, when it works

The certified path protects the numbers people are accountable to. The ad hoc path is what makes the client's stated AI-agent objective achievable at all — an agent cannot be limited to questions someone anticipated and precomputed. The ad hoc path is not a convenience feature; it is the objective. The reconciliation contract is what lets you have it without putting leadership reporting at risk.

05Executable definitions

Meaning and formula must agree. They are not the same artifact. The ontology authors what the term means; the Store catalog and the consumption semantic layer author how the certified number is computed. Collapsing those into one object is the C-10 failure mode.

The problem in one line

"Rack price capture rate is realised rack revenue as a percentage of the published benchmark" is a meaning statement. It cannot compile to SQL. The certified formula — expression, bindings, additivity, grain — lives in the Store catalog and is compiled only by Databricks Metric Views (programme; MetricFlow for demo 1 only). If a second formula is hand-written into a batch job or a Power BI model, you have two formulas, and the certified one becomes decorative within a quarter. Meaning and formula must agree; they must not be forced into one object.

The Silver metadata question, stated as a decision

If the Silver KPI-metadata table is independently editable, the C-10 split is gone and it will diverge from the Store catalog (and from ontology IRIs) within a quarter — because editing a table is easier than editing the catalog or the graph. If it is a one-way projection of the Store catalog plus ontology IRIs, the boundary holds and warehouse-native consumers still get what they need. This is DK-03, already locked by C-10, and it is decided before the table is built.

The practical test for any proposed edit path Ask: "if someone changes this here, where does that change flow to?" If the answer is "nowhere, it just sits here," the location is not authoritative and should be read-only. If the answer is "everywhere," it is the authoring home. Anything in between is where drift lives.

07Lineage — two kinds, both needed

The work currently in progress is producing both, and they are frequently conflated. They serve different questions, satisfy different audiences, and belong in different homes.

Definitional lineage

Why does this number mean anything?

KPI → domain IRIs (signed CV/CQ) → Constraint references → measures → business terms

Answers: what the terms mean, which constraint (if any) is referenced, who is accountable for the Threshold, what action a move triggers. Process linkage may explain why a number exists. It is not a certification path and not a substitute for domain IRIs or Constraint references.

Home: the ontology layer. Audience: business, governance, AI agents needing context. Enriched by: Workstream 2 domain models. Meaning is signed at G3/G4 CV/CQ — it is not a Business KPI Owner decision.

Data lineage

Where did this number come from?

KPI value → Gold → Silver → Raw → operational source system → field

Answers: what feeds this, what breaks it, what is affected if a source changes, can I trust the freshness.

Home: Unity Catalog and Purview, referenced from the ontology. Audience: engineering, data quality, impact analysis.

Why the distinction matters operationally

QuestionAnswered byIf only the other exists
"A source table changed — which KPIs are affected?"Data lineageYou find out from a broken dashboard
"Why are we measuring this at all?"Process / objective linkageProcess linkage explains existence; it is not a certification path and not definitional lineage. Definitional lineage answers what the term means (domain IRIs + Constraint refs)
"Two KPIs disagree — do they share a source?"Data lineageWeeks of investigation
"Two KPIs disagree — do they mean the same thing?"Definitional lineageYou reconcile numbers that were never supposed to match
"An agent is asked a question — which KPI applies?"BothThe agent picks a plausible-looking KPI and answers confidently and wrongly
Lineage should terminate at the source system, not at Raw

Stopping at the Raw layer answers "which table did this come from." Continuing to the operational system and field answers "which business event created this." Only the second supports impact analysis when an operational system changes, and only the second lets you tell a business person where their number actually originates. The extra hop is where most of the value is.

08Classification and validation

Approach position only. The rubric, the twelve-class taxonomy applied, and the certification workflow are EPM-KPI-020.

The sequencing question

The planned sequence is in-house SME review, then Store client validation (KPI-020). Both are right; what matters is what each is reviewing. Classification must come first, or the SMEs spend their time validating definitions for calculations that should never enter the store.

StepQuestion being answeredWhoOutput tier

The framing that makes step 4 productive

Present the SME review as a classification review first and a definition review second. "Is this a KPI?" is a faster, more decisive conversation than "is this definition right?", and answering it first removes most of the inventory from the second conversation. Running them in the other order means debating the precise wording of calculations that will be retired.

What Store client validation cannot do

Validation is evidence, not approval. A room agreeing that a definition looks right does not create an accountable owner, a target, a threshold, a review cadence, or a defined management action — and without those, the Charter's own qualification criteria are unmet. Approval requires a named person to accept accountability, which is a different conversation and often a harder one.

Method The classification rubric, the three-axis inventory taxonomy, the SME review and Store client validation (KPI-020) gate criteria, and the certification workflow are EPM-KPI-020 v0.3.

12Artifact update block

Conclusions

  • The KPI Store has two execution modes over one certified formula. Making them agree is the central architectural problem, and it needs a stated reconciliation contract with a continuous test — not a design assumption.
  • The consumption semantic layer (Databricks Metric Views) is the only programme compile path. MetricFlow compiles demo 1 only. Gold stores a versioned, immutable snapshot of that compile. Gold is the leadership number of record because it is a frozen snapshot, not because it owns the formula.
  • Meaning and formula must agree; they are not one object. Ontology authors meaning (signed at G3/G4 CV/CQ). Store catalog + consumption semantic layer author the certified formula. The ontology does not author formulas.
  • Non-additive measures aggregated across grain is the highest-severity divergence source and is preventable by one design rule: declare additivity, and make the programme compiler (Databricks Metric Views) refuse illegal aggregation. Demo 1 uses MetricFlow’s refusal.
  • Extraction is complete; classification is not. "All the KPIs" is almost certainly "all the calculations," and the expectation should be set before results land.
  • Two kinds of lineage are in progress and should not be conflated — definitional lineage is retargeted onto domain IRIs and Constraint references; data lineage explains origin. Process linkage may explain why a number exists; it is not a certification path.
  • LimitValue pattern; Constraint ≠ Threshold ≠ Breach ≠ Binding. Store KPIs reference constraints; they do not subclass them. Threshold authority = KPI owner. Constraint authority = physics / permit / spec / SOL. Store is not the Constraints owner.

Definitions added

  • Reconciliation contract — the six commitments that make two execution modes (compile and snapshot) one architecture.
  • Reproducibility assertion — given the same as-of time and definition version, the consumption semantic layer reproduces the Gold snapshot exactly.
  • Additivity declaration — a mandatory definition field stating whether and over which dimensions a measure may be aggregated.
  • Tier elevation — the framing of this workstream as moving extracted calculations from Tier 8 to Tier 1 through defined steps.
  • Definitional versus data lineage — meaning versus origin; different homes, different audiences.

Assumptions

  • The medallion layers are Databricks-based with Unity Catalog governance. Programme warehouse host is Azure (Databricks SQL warehouse on Azure; Synapse and Fabric named as options, not a bake-off). Demo 2 host is Databricks Free edition only — not Azure.
  • The extraction covers Tableau-derived logic; whether it covers Power BI-local measures is QK-12.
  • Divergence sources DV-1 to DV-9 are the general set; this estate may have others, particularly around unit-of-measure conversion.
  • Databricks Metric Views are named as the programme compiler; MetricFlow as the demo-1 compiler; Ossie as interchange. C-10 is not reopened — only the compiler tool changed. Hamid may reopen the tool again; until then this is locked.

13Glossary

TermWhat it actually means
MeasureA number with an agreed meaning. "Volume sold."
MetricA measure watched over time in context. "Volume sold, by terminal, by month."
KPIA metric with an accountable owner, a target, thresholds, a review cadence, and a defined action. Practical test: if nobody would do anything differently when it moves, it is not a KPI
AdditiveA measure you can safely add up across any dimension. Volume is additive; a percentage is not
Semi-additiveAddable across some dimensions but not others. Inventory adds across locations but not across time — you cannot sum January and February closing stock
Non-additiveCannot be aggregated by summing at all. Ratios, averages, distinct counts. Must be recomputed from components at the new grain
GrainThe level of detail a number is stored at. You can always aggregate up and never disaggregate down — which is why Gold should be stored atomically
PrecomputeCalculating and storing a value in advance. Fast and stable, but only answers questions you anticipated
Ad hoc computationGenerating the calculation at the moment the question is asked. Flexible, current, and the only way an agent can answer something nobody planned for
Ontology layerThe RDF/OWL model of meaning. SoT for what the term means. Does not author formulas. C-10 / C-11
Consumption semantic layerThe metric-serving layer that holds the certified formula and compiles it (Databricks Metric Views for the programme; MetricFlow for demo 1) to SQL on demand. SoT, with the Store catalog, for how the certified number is computed. Never say “semantic layer” unqualified
Store catalogThe certified catalog of Store KPIs — one name, one population, one formula, one grain. Not the ontology. Not the Measurement Catalog
Databricks Metric ViewsThe programme compiler (Unity Catalog Business Semantics). The only programme compile path. Hosted on a Databricks SQL warehouse
MetricFlowDemo 1 compiler only. Not the programme default
OssieApache Ossie (formerly OSI). Interchange only — not authoring, not a second formula, not OWL
Store lead · KPI owner · formula authorThe three Store seats. Store lead governs the catalog; KPI owner owns target / Threshold / cadence / action; formula author authors the certified formula. All Must where C-10 is decided
LimitValueShared n-ary pattern: value, unit, quantity kind, direction, reference conditions, valid-time. Reused by Constraint and Threshold; identity is not shared
Constraint ≠ Threshold ≠ Breach ≠ BindingConstraint authority = physics / permit / spec / SOL. Threshold authority = KPI owner. Store KPIs reference constraints; they do not subclass them (D-M18)
customer_grainRequired on Store-certified pilots. One of payer, sold-to, site. Do not overload Customer Account, Sold-To, Bill-To, Payer, Ship-To (location), or CreditAccount (on payer) as “Customer”
Compile to SQLTurning a stored KPI definition into a real query against the warehouse, automatically, rather than someone writing it by hand
As-of timeThe moment the data was true. The main reason two correct calculations produce two different numbers
RestatementRecalculating a published figure because the data or definition changed. Requires versioning, or last quarter's board pack becomes unreproducible
Effective datingRecording which version of a definition applied to which period, so history stays correct when meaning changes
CertifiedOne name, one population, one formula, one grain, versioned, consumption-SL-only compile, Gold snapshot. Owner act is necessary, not sufficient. Terms must already sit in a signed CV
FrozenWritten once and not recalculated. What makes an audit trail possible
Authoring homeThe one place a fact is created and changed. Everywhere else holds a generated copy. The rule that stops eight locations disagreeing
ProjectionA generated, refreshed copy of something authored elsewhere. Read-only by design
Definitional lineageWhy a number means something — the chain from KPI to domain IRIs and Constraint references. Process linkage may explain existence; it is not a certification path
Data lineageWhere a number came from — the chain from KPI value back to the source system field
Tier elevationMoving a calculation from unvalidated report logic up to approved enterprise KPI through defined governance steps
