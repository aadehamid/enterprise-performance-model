This is the ontology lane only. Domain models and the KPI Store hang off it; they are not this document. Nothing here invents a workshop, a gate, or a format. Where the planning docs name a form, that form is quoted. Where they say “document” or “register” without naming Word vs HTML, that gap is marked and a working recommendation is given.

Locks — compact, once
  • Customer = order-to-cash. Ops / Tech = plan-to-produce.
  • Pipeline (load-bearing): Controlled vocabulary → metadata standards → taxonomy → thesaurus (SKOS) → ontology → knowledge graph. Do not skip to graph-first.
  • Constraint ≠ Threshold ≠ Breach ≠ Binding. Shared LimitValue pattern. Store KPIs reference constraints; they do not subclass them.
  • C-10: ontology = meaning SoT. KPI Store + consumption SL = certified formula + compute. Warehouse / Gold = volume. Azure warehouse is physical, not meaning.
  • C-11: ontology layer is not a BI/compiler “semantic layer.” Programme compiler = Databricks (Metric Views / UC semantics). MetricFlow = existing Unbilled demo only.
  • Do not overload “Customer.” Party + Customer Account + Sold-To / Bill-To / Payer / Ship-To (location) / Loading-Authorized Party.
  • DP-18 Hamid slice gate: CW1–CW2 and P1 assessment are fine. CW11+ conceptual build, Turtle, and Purview publish wait on the slice.
  • Q-03 still open: client-owned deliverable vs internal technique. Branches handover, training, and who attends CW7.
  • Graph product (GraphDB vs Jena) is not picked. Neo4j, if used, is serving only — not a second meaning SoT.
  • No Teaching Guide.

04 Deliverables and their formats

DEL-002 §13 names a Form. It does not say PowerPoint or Word for most rows. Q-27 (documents, models, running systems, or all three) is still open and is asked at CW1. Every row below marks stated vs working recommendation.

Hamid — “Is this PowerPoint, Excel, Word?”
  • Workshop pre-reads and ratification packs: short documents (HTML or Word / PDF). Slides only if the client’s forum requires them — not the SoT.
  • Design standard and playbook: documents, not decks.
  • The ontology itself: Turtle + SHACL + CQs in git. That is the product.
  • Mapping and disposition: registers (tables), not slides.
  • Excel: staging only. Never the meaning SoT.
  • Purview: published terms (platform state), not an authored glossary.
  • Running store: only if Q-03 = client-owned and D-M11 picks an operable platform.
