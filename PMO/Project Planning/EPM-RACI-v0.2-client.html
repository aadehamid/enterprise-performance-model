Letters

How the letters work

A blank cell means that role is not in the activity. Where a source table named two people as the decider, this matrix keeps one A and records the other as R or C.

R

Responsible. Does the work. At least one per row. More than one R is allowed when the work is shared.

A

Accountable. Signs off. Exactly one A per row.

C

Consulted. Two-way input before the work.

I

Informed. One-way update.

A/R

Same person is Accountable and Responsible. Allowed.

Every row has at least one R and exactly one A. A+R on the same person is allowed. This matrix is a draft until CW1 / CK1 walks it with the named people.

Roles

Fifteen standing columns

Columns are roles, not named people. Names land in the CW1 / CK1 nomination table. Day-to-day counterpart unblocks access and diaries. They help on the access row. They are not a standing decision column. Approval forum chair convenes the certification act. The forum is still open (QD-05 / DEP-03). They are C on the certification row until the forum is named.

Sponsor

Executive sponsor. Scope, resourcing, escalations, scale, and the mobilisation gates.

EPM Lead

Architectural coherence, which facts are meaning (models and ontology) and which are volume (Store numbers), O2C and P2P interfaces, artifact acceptance. Confirmed.

DDO Cust

Yes

Domain Data Owner - Customer. Every customer definition. Signs the Customer CV and CQs. Promotes the Customer model (G3).

Coord Constr

Yes

Coordinating Domain Data Owner - Constraints. Structure, taxonomy, and lifecycle. Not individual constraint values. Signs the Constraints CV and CQs. Promotes the Constraints model (G4).

EDG

Enterprise Data Governance lead. Standards ratification, classification criteria, term-publication policy.

GPO

Global Process Owner. Cross-domain boundary disputes only. If no GPO exists at kickoff, the sponsor takes those dispute rows.

Store lead

KPI Store lead. Store catalog, C-10 (certified formula lives in the catalog and the consumption semantic layer), certification bar, formula-authoring home. Named by CW2 / CK1.

KPI owner

Yes (Store)

Business KPI Owner. One per candidate KPI. Target, Threshold, cadence, and the action taken. Does not author meaning or the certified formula.

DM Lead

Domain Modeling Lead. Method, standards, workshop facilitation. Not the same person as a modeler. Still to confirm (Q-21).

Ontologist

Ontology modules, validation shapes, signed CQs, meaning SoT. Confirmed.

Formula

Formula author (Store / analytics engineer). Authors the certified number in the consumption semantic layer. Distinct from Store lead and KPI owner. Named before CW10 (DP-16).

Backend

Backend / data engineers. Graph operation, Silver projection, metadata sync, Gold snapshots. Confirmed.

EA

Enterprise architect. Wider platform direction. Joint recommend on graph persistence.

Digital

Client digital and engineering. Decides graph persistence and Metric Views / compiler operability.

Platform

Platform owners as a group: ER/Studio admin, Purview owner, Databricks / UC engineer, graph platform owner (if one exists), security / access administrator.

  • Independent reviewer at G3 / G4 / GK1 / GK2. Named as a class, not a person.
  • Domain Modeling Lead and the two business analysts (Q-21). Facilitation seat still open.
  • Classification analysts, review facilitator, and session coordinator (DEL-003 section 08).
  • Formula author, Store lead, and KPI owner must be three people. Do not collapse at CK1.
  • GPO may not exist. If none at kickoff, the sponsor takes those dispute rows.
  • AI / agent initiative lead (QD-11). Include only if one exists.
  • Graph platform owner only if a platform exists. Local files are enough for the foundations gate (G2).
  • BigEye owner is advisory. Not a column.
  • Security / access is inside Platform. Raise the request in week 1.
