# core changelog

## 2.2.0 (2026-10-22)

A minor release: it adds concepts and notes, and removes no IRI. Built from the released 2.1.0 graph; 2.1.0 is unchanged. It carries the process-map changes merged since 2.0.0 was built (#288 to #296 and #301), read from the map at commit 8259354, the merge of #301. The decision owner approved the release plan that names it: "release plan approved" (EPM-DEC-001-0076; their words in `step4/review-evidence/decision-owner-answers/answers-2026-10-22-morning.md`).

- [minor] **23 new process concepts** from the map: CM 1.1.3.7.13 Reconcile Movement Losses and Gains and CM 1.2.5.1.5 Manage Renewable Feedstock Traceability and Carbon-Intensity Evidence (EPM-DEC-001-0048); CM 1.2.4.3.8 Manage Environmental Credits and Clean-Fuel Incentives with its two children (MPC-P05); RF 1.2.1 to RF 1.2.5, the turnaround coordination steps (MPC-P02b); and 13 Midstream service concepts, MS 1.1 to MS 1.4 with their children (MPC-P01), all under EPM-DEC-001-0047. Each is shaped as 2.1.0's map-sourced concepts are: preferred label, notation, definition, parent, scheme and taxonomy level. The definition is the first sentence of the map's description, the rest is the scope note, and a provenance note names the map commit and the record that added it.
- [minor] **IDs on four stubs.** RF 1, RF 1.1, RF 1.2 and MS 1 keep their IRIs (`L1-refining`, `L2-refinery-performance-and-risk-coordination`, `L2-refinery-asset-reliability-and-turnaround-coordination`, `L1-midstream`) and gain their IDs as `skos:notation`.
- [minor] **Changed descriptions as scope notes** on eight concepts: CM 1.1.7 and CM 1.2.5, whose definitions are the map's earlier descriptions, get the added text (MPC-P03, P04); CM 1.2.4.3.6 (MPC-P05), CM 1.2.5.1 (EPM-DEC-001-0051), CM 1.3.3.4 (MPC-P07), CM 1.3.8.4.6 (MPC-P08), MS 1 (MPC-P01) and RF 1.2 (MPC-P02b) get the map's new description. Approved definitions are unchanged.
- [patch] **APQC element 19948** (EPM-DEC-001-0060). Sixteen concepts reference "Analyze internal operations" (APQC 1.1.3.2), their top-scoring step 3b candidate. Fourteen marked "No candidate" are confirmed: the reference names the candidate compared, and the decision says it does not match. CM 1.2.6.3 Supply Operations and the scheme root Downstream Operations were "Review link"; both are corrected to "Rejected", each with a change note.
- [patch] **RF 1.2 is no longer a Candidate node** (EPM-DEC-001-0063, item 2): MPC-P02b populated it and its ownership question is answered for the map's scope. A change note says so; its 2026-09-22 provenance stays as history.
- The shapes and the canonical-fact mapping are 2.1.0's, unchanged.
