## Definitions

- **Restates-hierarchy relation:** a relationship mention whose only content
  repeats the parent-child structure (e.g. child `governed-by` parent, child
  `enables` parent). Held, not emitted — it adds little and risks
  double-counting in queries. Precedent: REL-00366 (Hamid 2026-09-24).
- **Source-meaning rule:** emission may emit, hold, or defer a mention. It
  never swaps a verb or retargets a relationship. Corrections are made in the
  source workbook through a reviewed authoring change, then flow through the
  pipeline normally.
