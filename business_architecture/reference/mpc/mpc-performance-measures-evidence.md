# MPC performance measures: definitions, grain, compensation link, and peer comparison

**Status:** Draft evidence · **Prepared:** 2026-10-18 · **Used for:** the `kpi` module's measure classification (EPM-DEC-001-0042) · **Scope:** Marathon Petroleum Corporation (MPC) and MPLX LP public disclosures for FY2025 and 2026 to date, compared with the FY2025 10-Ks of Valero, Phillips 66, PBF Energy and HF Sinclair.

MPC publicly defines about twenty refining, logistics and safety measures. Five of them drive the 2025 and 2026 annual cash bonus: two peer-relative refining measures, adjusted EBITDA, MPLX distributable cash flow per unit, and a safety, environmental and human-capital scorecard. Margin capture, utilization, operating cost per barrel and crack spreads are reported but are not bonus metrics. MPC does not publicly report mechanical availability, an Energy Intensity Index (EII) or marine utilization.

Source IDs follow `business_architecture/reference/mpc/source-register.md` (MPC-S01, MPC-S03, MPC-S07, MPLX-S01). Sources first used here are added to `source-register.md` as MPC-S16 to MPC-S21. Rows marked "Not confirmed" in the source list may not carry a fact on their own (source-register rule). Every quote below is verbatim from the filing named. "Not confirmed" marks anything not read in a primary source.

## Findings that settle open items in the repo

1. **Utilization uses average capacity, not year-end capacity.** This settles the open "Capacity basis" item in `mpc-fy2025-10k-fact-sheet.md` and note 4 in `pricing-domain-evidence.md`. The 10-K footnote says utilization is "Based on calendar-day capacity, which is an annual average that includes down time for planned maintenance and other normal operating activities" (MPC-S01, Item 7, Supplemental R&M Statistics, p. 53). The Q4 2025 release shows 2,963 mbpcd as the 2025 capacity next to 94% (MPC-S07, p. 9). Check: 2,787 mbpd crude ÷ 2,963 = 94.1%. The year-end figure of 2,986 mbpcd becomes the capacity basis in 2026 (MPC-S17 shows 2,986 for Q2 2026).
2. **Regional R&M margin per barrel is confirmed.** FY2025: Gulf Coast $14.82, Mid-Continent $17.27, West Coast $20.57 per barrel (MPC-S07, pp. 10–11). This settles note 3 in `pricing-domain-evidence.md`. The regional dollar margins ($18,322M total) exclude the LIFO inventory adjustment; the segment total is $18,404M (MPC-S07, p. 16, footnote (b)).
3. **The region names are MPC's own.** "Mid-Continent" covers the northern and Rockies refineries as well as El Paso. MPC-S01 Item 2 (p. 33) lists:
   - Gulf Coast (1,248 mbpcd): Galveston Bay (Texas City, TX) 631; Garyville, LA 617.
   - Mid-Continent (1,186 mbpcd): Catlettsburg, KY 307; Robinson, IL 253; Detroit, MI 146; El Paso, TX 133; St. Paul Park, MN 105; Canton, OH 100; Mandan, ND 72; Salt Lake City, UT 70.
   - West Coast (552 mbpcd): Los Angeles, CA 365; Anacortes, WA 119; Kenai, AK 68.
4. **Margin capture dropped out of the Q2 2026 release.** The Q4 2025 release defines and reports capture (105% for FY2025). The Q2 2026 release (MPC-S17) has no capture definition or figure. The Q1 2026 release was not read.
5. **The R&M margin definition changed in 2026.** It now adds "which includes impacts from derivative activity" (MPC-S03, Non-GAAP Financial Measures; MPC-S17). The FY2025 10-K wording does not have it. This repeats E18 in `pricing-domain-evidence.md` and is now confirmed in the Q2 2026 release as well.

## 2. Peer comparison (FY2025 10-Ks)

All four peers define a realized margin, a throughput and an operating-cost measure. They differ on four points: the per-barrel denominator, the treatment of turnarounds, how they name regions, and which crack benchmark they use. Peer figures come from the 10-K only. Valero, for example, publishes per-barrel and regional data in its earnings releases, which were not read.

**What the comparison means for the model.**
- "Utilization" needs its capacity basis attached. MPC uses calendar-day average capacity, HF Sinclair uses stream-day capacity, and PSX uses crude capacity by region. Values above 100% (PSX Central Corridor 102%, HF Sinclair Mid-Con 102.7%) follow from those bases.
- "Margin per barrel" needs its denominator attached: net throughput (MPC), processed inputs (PSX), throughput (PBF) or produced barrels sold (HF Sinclair).
- Turnaround cost per barrel is only comparable after adjusting for accounting policy. MPC itself gives that as its reason for excluding turnarounds from adjusted EBITDA (MPC-S07, p. 14).
- MPC's bonus measure "Refining EBITDA per barrel of total throughput" by region is compared against PSX and Valero. Neither peer's 10-K publishes a regional EBITDA per barrel; PSX publishes regional pre-tax income per barrel. How MPC builds peer-equivalent regions is **not disclosed**.

## 3. Discrepancies and gaps

- **Pipeline throughput bases differ.** MPC reports 6,067 mbpd (FY2025). MPLX reports crude 3,899 plus products 2,066, which is 5,965 mbpd "transported for" MPC and third parties. The footnotes differ: MPC counts "common-carrier pipelines and private pipelines contributed to MPLX". This note does not reconcile the 102 mbpd gap.
- **R&M operating expense reconciliation differs between documents.** The 10-K shows operating expenses (excluding D&A) of $11,970M. The Q4 2025 release shows $11,817M. Both reach the same R&M margin ($18,404M). Not investigated.
- **Regional utilization** is not published. Any regional utilization in the model is a derivation and should be labelled as one.
- **Sustainability metrics** (#24, #26 unit, #33) could not be read on marathonpetroleum.com, which returned HTTP 403. Re-read them from the MPC 2025 Sustainability Report and Performance Data before citing them as evidence (source-register rule: "search excerpt only" sources may not be cited).
- **The R&M Margin Indicator formula** (the denominator of capture) is published only as monthly market data on the MPC investor website. It was not read.
- **Peer 10-K page numbers** come from the page markers in the EDGAR HTML. Check them against the PDFs before quoting by page.
