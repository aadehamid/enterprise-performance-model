# Losses and gains, and renewable feedstock traceability: regulators and standards

**Status:** Evidence for EPM-DEC-001-0048 · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-19

This file ranks below MPC's own statements (`../mpc/loss-gain-and-feedstock-evidence.md`), which Hamid ruled take precedence. The regulators' rules set what the feedstock traceability step must keep. The industry standards are support only.

## Regulators

| ID | Source | What it says (quoted) | Supports |
|---|---|---|---|
| RG-01 | 40 CFR 80.1454(b)(3), eCFR, up to date as of 10/15/2026, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-80/subpart-M/section-80.1454 | A renewable fuel producer keeps records "related to the generation and assignment of RINs for each facility", including "( vi ) Type and quantity of feedstocks used." | Feedstock type and quantity per facility |
| RG-02 | 40 CFR 80.1454(j), same page | A producer that uses "biogenic waste oils/fats/greases" keeps "Documents demonstrating the amounts, by weight, purchased" and "Documents demonstrating the location of any establishment(s) from which the waste stream ... is collected." | Waste-oil feedstock quantity and collection location |
| RG-03 | California Air Resources Board, "2025 LCFS Amendment Implementation FAQ", https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/resources/fact-sheets/2025-lcfs-amendment-implementation-faq/printable/print | The sustainability requirements "added to the regulation under new subsection 95488.9(g)" became "effective July 1, 2025"; holders of pathways using biomass covered by section 95488.9(g)(1)(A) "must maintain attestations as specified by section 95488.9(g)(5)(C)"; a verifier's sampling plan describes "whether a feedstock tracking platform is used" and reviews "chain of custody documentation, and information from upstream suppliers". | LCFS feedstock attestations and chain-of-custody evidence |
| RG-04 | Internal Revenue Service, proposed regulations, "Section 45Z Clean Fuel Production Credit", 91 FR 5160 (2026-02-04), https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-02-04/html/2026-02246.htm | Proposed section 1.45Z-4(f)(1): fuel produced after December 31, 2025 "must be exclusively derived from a feedstock that was produced or grown in the United States, Mexico, or Canada". Proposed section 1.45Z-4(g): records "sufficient to establish the taxpayer's eligibility"; under (g)(1), records "establishing any relevant information relating to the primary feedstock(s) used to produce each fuel". | 45Z feedstock origin and feedstock records. A proposed rule, not final |

## Industry standards (support only)

| ID | Source | What it says (quoted) | Supports |
|---|---|---|---|
| SD-01 | API, Petroleum Measurement catalog (2023), https://www.api.org/-/media/files/publications/2023_catalog/petroleum_measurement-2023.pdf | MPMS Chapter 17.5: "intended to provide a basis for analyzing and reconciling the quantity differences (gains/losses) resulting from marine custody transfer movement(s)"; Chapter 23, Reconciliation of Hydrocarbon Quantities: "Provides practical methodologies for monitoring hydrocarbon transportation loss and gain for non-marine systems i.e. pipeline, tank cars" | Loss and gain reconciliation is a standard practice for every mode |
| SD-02 | ISCC EU 203 Traceability and Chain of Custody, version 4.2, valid from 21 May 2025, https://www.iscc-system.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ISCC_EU_203_Traceability_and_Chain-of-Custody_4.2.pdf | Companies keep "Records of incoming and outgoing sustainable products (e.g. weighbridge tickets, bill of lading and Sustainability Declarations)" and "Quantity bookkeeping for sustainable and non-sustainable material and, if applicable, a mass balance calculation" | The chain-of-custody records the traceability step keeps |

SD-01 quotes the catalog's descriptions, not the paywalled standards themselves; SD-02 is a certification scheme's document, cited as practice, not as a rule MPC is shown to follow.
