# Customer classification: U.S. refining peers (secondary)

**Status:** Evidence · **Prepared:** 2026-10-21 · **Checks:** `../mpc/customer-classification-evidence.md` (MPC's classification C1 to C8 and its facets, adopted by the decision owner on 2026-09-29)

## What the filings say

- **Valero:** "We sell refined petroleum products in both the wholesale rack and bulk markets." and "We sell our products on a wholesale basis through an extensive rack marketing network." This matches MPC's contract-type facet (wholesale/rack) and its delivery tier (bulk sales).
- **Phillips 66:** "Our refined products are marketed on both a branded and unbranded basis." "In the Gulf Coast and East Coast regions, most sales are conducted via the unbranded channel of trade, which does not require a highly integrated marketing network to secure petroleum product placement for refinery pull through." "Our wholesale operations utilize a network of marketers operating approximately 5,360 outlets." It also reports brand licensing: "In addition, we hold brand-licensing agreements covering approximately 1,460 sites." This matches MPC's brand-status facet, and its marketers operating outlets play the role MPC's brand jobbers (C1) play.
- **PBF Energy:** "Products produced at the Delaware City refinery are transferred to customers through pipelines, barges or at its truck rack." PBF names the delivery mode, which MPC's classification does not carry as a facet.
- **HF Sinclair:** "Our principal customers for gasoline include other refiners, branded sites, convenience store chains, independent marketers and retailers." "Our marketing operations include branded fuel sales to more than 1,700 branded sites in the United States and licensing fees for the use of the Sinclair brand to more than 350 additional locations throughout the United States." Other refiners as customers fit MPC's bulk sales "to large unbranded resellers and other downstream companies" (`../mpc/customer-classification-evidence.md`, section 2).

## This file's reading

These are not the filings' words. The peers sort customers on the axes MPC uses (sales channel, brand status, rack against bulk), and nothing in the quotes conflicts with C1 to C8. Two additions are candidates for the Customer work (Phase C), for the decision owner to accept or leave out:

- **A facet: delivery mode** (pipeline, barge, truck rack), from PBF.
- **A relationship: brand licensing.** Phillips 66 and HF Sinclair both report brand-licensing agreements. HF Sinclair counts its licensed locations apart from the branded sites it sells fuel to; reading those as sites it does not supply is this file's inference. MPC's classes do not list a licensing relationship.
