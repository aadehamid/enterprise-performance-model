What the check found#

MPC's refineries move intermediate products between sites, including during partial shutdowns (reference/mpc/mpc-fy2025-10k-fact-sheet.md, Item 1 operating facts). The decision owner ruled "P03: A with a physical-execution coverage check" (step4/review-evidence/decision-owner-answers/answers-2026-10-18.md). This check carries out that condition. It goes through the steps a transfer passes from nomination to settlement; rows 2 to 6 are its physical execution, and rows 1 and 7 frame it. CM 1.1.7's description now names the existing steps.

Assumption behind "covered": CM 1.1.3 (Distribution Planning & Scheduling and Inventory Management) handles intermediates as well as finished products. Its inventory steps include "Develop Intermediates Inventory Policy" (CM 1.1.3.7.2), which supports that reading. If the decision owner reads CM 1.1.3 as finished products only, the rows that rest on CM 1.1.3 alone become gaps: nomination and scheduling, and movement execution. Quantity measurement keeps CM 1.2.3, receipt and reconciliation keep CM 1.1.5.1.2.5, and settlement keeps CM 1.2.4.1.18, because those sit outside CM 1.1.3.

What the decision owner decides#

  1. Loss and gain. Decided 2026-10-19: The decision owner said to address the gap (EPM-DEC-001-0048); option A was applied. (A, recommended) Add one step, "Reconcile Movement Losses and Gains", under CM 1.1.3.7 Inventory, covering every movement, not only inter-refinery transfers; it produces the Loss Control data product. (B) Record loss and gain as part of CM 1.1.3.6.4 Discharge & Reconcile Load, by description only. (C) Leave the gap open.
  2. Quality of transferred intermediates. Decided 2026-10-19: the decision owner chose "Widen CM 1.2.5.1 (Recommended)" (EPM-DEC-001-0051); option A was applied. (A, recommended) Widen CM 1.2.5.1's description so it covers intermediates received as feed from another refinery. (B) Add a step under CM 1.1.7. (C) Leave it.

A is recommended for 1 because the data product already exists and needs a producing process (Step 7 integration), and one generic step avoids a transfer-only duplicate. A is recommended for 2 because a received intermediate is feed to the receiving refinery, which is the scope of CM 1.2.5.1.

Why nothing was added#

The decision owner's ruling set the check as a condition. The map gets no new process from a gap the check finds unless they rule on it (EPM-DEC-001-0047, What follows, item 4).
