# Known open items

What is open on the ontology and the demo, and what this release did not run. They are yours to close, or to leave, in your own copy: `HOW-TO-UPDATE.md` says how to change anything here.

- **The ontology's open items** are listed in `business_architecture/ontology/OPEN-ITEMS.md`: the points only the business can settle (who owns each module's meaning, the Market Data mandate, the PIDX codes, three role definitions, the measures' scope in business words, the internal systems' descriptions), the gaps the process map still records, and the work that needs no one from the business.
- **The pricing demo's acceptance tests** stopped at 2 of 21 use cases on the last recorded run, as the demo's README reports; the recorded runs and their re-judged verdicts ship in `demos/downstream-pricing-databricks-free/data/acceptance/`. The live run was not run on this release: it needs a Databricks workspace and its model endpoints, and it was the one heavy use of the workspace's daily limit.
- **The changelogs do not all meet the ontology skill's Known gaps convention.** The skill's `check_changelogs.py` requires a Known gaps section that says `None.` or gives `Reason:` and `Record:` values for each gap. Some packaged changelogs lack the section; others have gaps without those fields. This check is not part of `check.sh`. Aligning the packages is a change to the sources, for a later release.
- **Store sync is manual.** `business_architecture/ontology/projection/sync_stores.py check` says which store is behind the newest release, and `sync` reloads it; nothing runs it on a schedule here. The guide's "The in-sync strategy" lists what can drift and what catches it.
- **Technical-graph collection and sync are manual.** The `technical-graph` vocabulary ships in this release. The demo's `collect` reads Unity Catalog lineage, `build` creates the graph, and `lineage` compares the observed, designed and SQL lineage. `tech_graph sync` copies the graph from Neo4j to Databricks. `sync_stores.py sync --cloud` runs that copy when it is behind. See `demos/downstream-pricing-databricks-free/README.md` for the demo commands. See `business_architecture/ontology/projection/README.md` for `sync_stores.py`. No scheduled collection or sync is included.
