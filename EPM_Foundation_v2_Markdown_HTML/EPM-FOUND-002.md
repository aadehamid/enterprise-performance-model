## Purpose
This artifact explains how the organization moves from business intent to governed performance information and then to reusable data and consumption interfaces.

## Performance architecture
```text
Strategic Objective
        ↓ evaluated by
Enterprise KPI
        ↓ explained by
Performance Indicators and Diagnostic Metrics
        ↓ calculated from
Derived and Raw Measurements
        ↓ generated by
Processes, Activities, Events, and Decisions
```

KPIs are a performance overlay. They do not form the next level beneath activities in the business-architecture hierarchy.

## Data architecture
```text
Source Systems
    ↓
Raw / Bronze
    ↓
Silver: standardized and conformed business facts
    ↓
Gold: integrated and decision-ready data products
    ↓
Gold KPI Store: governed KPI definitions and values
    ↓
Semantic / Consumption Layer: approved views only
    ↓
Power BI semantic models, reports, APIs, analytics, automation, AI
```

## Cross-architecture relationships
| From | Relationship | To |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | evaluated by | KPI |
| KPI | explained by | Metric |
| Metric | calculated from | Measurement |
| Process or activity | generates | Measurement |
| Decision | influences | Outcome |
| Foundational data product | supplies | Derived data product |
| Derived data product | calculates or supplies | Metrics and KPI inputs |
| KPI Store | persists | Governed KPI values, targets, thresholds, and status |
| Semantic view | exposes | Governed business data to consumers |
| Power BI semantic model | organizes | Relationships, measures, hierarchies, formatting, and security |
| Report or AI application | consumes | Semantic views and semantic models |

## Role of the KPI Store
The KPI Store is a governed repository, not the complete Enterprise Performance Model.

It should hold:

- approved KPI identity and version;
- value and time grain;
- dimensional context;
- target and thresholds;
- status;
- effective dates;
- calculation and source lineage;
- load and reconciliation metadata.

It should not hold every extracted Tableau calculation, every source-system measure, or every report helper.

## Role of the semantic layer
The existing SQL semantic layer is the governed consumption boundary. Consumers access only approved views.

This physical layer can include:

- semantic views exposing foundational or derived data products;
- enterprise KPI views;
- security-filtered consumer contracts;
- stable naming and conformed identifiers;
- selected reusable calculations.

The semantic layer is not identical to the enterprise semantic model. It implements the subset of enterprise semantics needed for data consumption.

## Power BI semantic models
A Power BI semantic model sits on top of approved semantic-layer views and adds analytical behavior such as:

- table relationships;
- reusable DAX measures;
- hierarchies;
- formatting;
- time intelligence;
- row-level security where appropriate;
- business descriptions;
- drill paths.

Core enterprise logic should not be independently redefined in multiple Power BI models.

## Implementation principle
> Implement logic at the lowest sensible reusable governed layer.

- Source-specific cleansing belongs in Silver.
- Cross-source business integration and reusable row-level logic belong in Gold data products.
- Official KPI results, targets, thresholds, and versions belong in the KPI Store.
- Stable consumer contracts belong in semantic-layer views.
- Dynamic analytical behavior and presentation-oriented calculations belong in Power BI semantic models.
- Visual-only logic belongs in reports.

## End-to-end example: Gasoline Netback CPG
```text
Business objective
Maximize realized commercial margin
        ↓
KPI
Gasoline Netback CPG
        ↓ explained by
Sales price, product cost, transportation, RIN/ethanol, terminal and mix variances
        ↓ supplied by
Sales Transaction, Market Price, Transportation Cost,
Renewable Compliance Cost, Product, Location
        ↓ integrated in
Commercial Margin / Netback derived data product
        ↓ persisted as official result in
KPI Store
        ↓ exposed through
CONSUMPTION.v_kpi_values and Commercial Margin semantic views
        ↓ consumed by
Power BI executive scorecard and diagnostic analysis
```
